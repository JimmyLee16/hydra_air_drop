```markdown
# Rock Paper Scissor Tournament - Core Mechanism

## Basic Structure
- **Format:** Single-elimination bracket
- **Entry Fee:** 
  - 10 ADA or 100 RPS tokens
  - Free entry for stakers (500 RPS = 1 ticket)

## Prize Distribution
| Round | Winners | Prize Pool Allocation |
|-------|---------|-----------------------|
| Final | Champion | 50% of pool |
| Semi-Final | Runner-up | 20% of pool |
| Quarter-Final | Top 4 | 15% of pool |
| Early Rounds | Top 8 | 10% of pool |
| System Fee | - | 5% (2.5% burn, 2.5% treasury) |

## Game Rules
- Best of 3 matches
- 15-second move timer
- Chainlink VRF for randomness
- Anti-bot detection system

## Special Features
- Double-elimination for monthly finals
- Comeback multiplier (extra prizes for winning after 3 losses)
```
